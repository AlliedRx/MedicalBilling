Who can apply

You qualify if all of these are true.

  • You are 18 or older, or you have a high school diploma or GED
  • You are a new student in an AlliedRx certification program
  • You are paying your own tuition, in full or on a payment plan

Students whose tuition is paid by WIOA, military or veterans benefits, or an employer are not eligible. Those enrollments pay standard tuition.

How it works

There is no contest and no waiting for a decision.

  1. Fill out the free application below. No payment or card information is asked for.
  2. If you meet the requirements and scholarships are still available, you are awarded the scholarship right away.
  3. Register for your program. The scholarship rate is applied to your tuition.

Official rules

  • Award: a tuition reduction equal to the difference between standard tuition and the scholarship tuition rate, from $705 to $1,674 depending on the program. No cash is paid.
  • Number of awards: 100 scholarships.
  • Deadline: December 15, 2026, or when all 100 scholarships are awarded, whichever comes first.
  • How recipients are chosen: first come, first served. The first 100 applicants who meet the eligibility requirements are awarded.
  • Application fee: none.
  • Limits: one program per student. Cannot be combined with other discounts. Does not cover the registration fee, books, supplies or exam fees.
  • Withdrawals: refunds follow the AlliedRx refund policy, based on the tuition actually charged.
  • Changes: AlliedRx may change or end this scholarship for future applicants. Students already awarded keep their award.
